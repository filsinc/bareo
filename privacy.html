Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

Information we collect

When you visit Bareo or place an order, we may collect information such as your name, email address, shipping address, and payment details. Payment details are processed securely by our payment provider; we do not store your full card details ourselves.

How we use it

We use this information to process and ship your order, respond to your inquiries, and, where you've opted in, send you updates about products and promotions.

Sharing

We share information only as needed to fulfill your order, for example with our shipping supplier and payment processor. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar tools (including the Meta Pixel) to understand site traffic and measure the performance of our advertising.
